Privacy policy

16 November 2026

What the app stores

CommsPath stores the information you enter on your device. This can include the person’s name, therapist details, assessment responses, scores, notes and communication recommendations, plus any photos or audio recordings you choose to add. The app uses this information to save your work, resume assessments and prepare reports.

Storage and sharing

CommsPath does not require an account or send assessment records to CommsPath servers. The app does not use advertising or tracking SDKs. Device backups may include app data, depending on your settings. If you export a PDF, the destination you choose, such as email or cloud storage, may retain a copy under its own policies. Practice texts and calls are simulated within the app.

Camera, photos and microphone

The app requests access when you choose a feature that uses the camera, photo library or microphone. You can manage these permissions in your device settings. If you use system features such as dictation, the device provider handles those features under its own settings and privacy terms.

Keeping and deleting records

Saved assessments remain on the device until you delete them. Use Assessment History to delete saved assessments and their associated app copies of photos and recordings. You can also remove local app data by deleting the app. Separately exported files and backups must be managed where they are stored.

Questions about your information

If a therapist or care provider used CommsPath as part of your care, contact them to ask about access, corrections or deletion of your assessment record. They can explain how they use and retain it. In the UK, you can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.
